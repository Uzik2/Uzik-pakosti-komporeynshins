# Privacy Policy for Rick Bot

**Last updated:** 19.11.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Rick bot handles your data.

## 1. Operation Requirements
To function properly, the bot requires the server to have a text channel named **#crazy-rick-bot-channel**. The bot reads messages specifically in this channel to generate responses.

## 2. Data We Process
The bot reads the text messages you send to it in order to generate a response. It may also store your Discord User ID to remember the context of the conversation.

## 3. How Data is Used
Your text is sent to Google AI (Gemini) to generate a reply. **Important:** Because the bot uses Google's free tier API, the text you send may be used by Google to improve and train their AI models (according to Google's terms for free services). Therefore, **do not send any personal or sensitive information** (passwords, addresses, phone numbers, etc.) to the bot.

## 4. Data Storage
We do not maintain a database to permanently store your chat history. Messages are processed in real-time. Temporary logs may be kept by Google according to their privacy policy.
